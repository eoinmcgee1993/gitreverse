| “Remove inputs and dopamine receptors will upregulate on a predictable timeline.” | **Partially supported / oversimplified** | Receptor adaptations are studied in specific addiction and experimental contexts; this does not establish a generalised effect from reducing everyday digital stimulation. | Avoid receptor claims and neurochemical promises. |

## Evidence quality rules for future generated reports

1. Cite the original study or guideline, not just a competitor's citation summary.
2. Preserve population, intervention, comparator, outcome, duration and study type when available.
3. Mark missing full-text verification as unknown.
4. Never treat a correlation, animal study, biomarker, or acute laboratory finding as proof of a consumer programme outcome.
5. If sources conflict, retain both records and explain the disagreement rather than silently choosing one.
6. Confidence measures confidence that the report accurately represents the source; it is not a probability that the intervention works.
