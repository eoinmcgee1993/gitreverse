# Commercial assets: Attention Field Manual

## Persona and positioning

**Primary persona:** “The overloaded operator.” Adult with too many inputs, frequent task switching and a recurring intention-action gap. They want a concrete system, not another endless content feed or a promise of a brain reset.

**Job to be done:** “Help me make it easier to start and return to the work I chose, even when my motivation is unreliable.”

**Differentiation:** observable behaviour over neurochemical claims; small environment changes over heroic willpower; recovery plans over perfect streaks; a clear 30-day practice window followed by a 60-day maintenance plan.

**Brand tone:** direct, grounded, slightly irreverent, never shaming or pseudo-clinical.

### What this is
A self-guided workbook for people who know what they want to do but keep getting pulled away before they get started. Use short exercises to map your triggers, adjust your environment, practise starting and returning, and design a realistic plan for the next two months.

### What you get
- **30-day Field Manual:** four phases, short daily practices and troubleshooting.
- **30 action cards:** one practical cue for each day.
- **One-page tracker:** record attempts and observations without turning life into a punishment spreadsheet.
- **60-day maintenance guide:** keep the useful changes and adapt when circumstances change.
- **Evidence notes:** clear references and plain-language limits on what the research does and does not show.

### The four phases
1. See it: identify patterns without self-judgement.
2. Cut friction: make the chosen action easier and distractions less automatic.
3. Operate: practise focused blocks, starts and restarts.
4. Preserve: plan for missed days and build a sustainable continuation.

### Who it is for
People who want a practical structure for attention and everyday habits, and who prefer written, self-directed tools.

### Who it is not for
This is not a diagnostic test, medical treatment, ADHD treatment, addiction treatment, or a replacement for professional care. It does not promise changes to dopamine, receptors or any other biomarker.

### FAQ
**Will I form a permanent habit in 30 days?**  
There is no universal timeline. Thirty days gives you a structured period to practise and observe. Habit strength varies by behaviour, person and context.

**What if I miss a day?**  
Continue with the next useful action. Do not double up or restart the whole programme. A missed day is data about friction or circumstances, not a verdict on you.

**Does this treat ADHD or anxiety?**  
No. It is an educational self-management resource and does not replace diagnosis, medication, therapy or clinical advice. If you have a condition or significant distress, involve a qualified clinician.

**How much time does it take?**  
Most practices are designed to take 5–20 minutes. You can use the low-energy versions when needed.

**Is it a video course?**  
No. It is a downloadable written toolkit with action cards, a tracker and maintenance guide.

**Is the outcome guaranteed?**  
No. Results depend on context and individual circumstances. The goal is to help you run a practical experiment and decide what is useful for you.

### CTA close
You do not need another theory about what is wrong with you. You need a small action, a clear cue and a plan for what happens when the day goes sideways.

**Start the 30-day practice. Keep what works. Change what doesn't.**

## Pricing hypotheses and validation plan

Do not assume competitor pricing validates demand. Test one price variable at a time:
- **Test A:** $19 starter toolkit (manual + tracker).
- **Test B:** $29 complete toolkit (manual + cards + tracker + 60-day guide).
- **Test C:** $39 complete toolkit plus future version updates.
- Optional future bundle: $49 only after interviews and paid demand justify it.

Validation steps:
1. Conduct 8–12 short interviews with target buyers; ask about existing workarounds and what they paid for, not just whether they “like” the idea.
2. Publish a landing page with one clear offer and transparent deliverables.
3. Track qualified visits, email opt-ins, checkout starts, purchases and refunds only with privacy-appropriate analytics.
4. Run price tests sequentially or with a valid split test; don't compare different traffic sources as if they were equivalent.
5. Interview buyers and non-buyers about the clarity and usefulness of the toolkit.
6. Do not claim efficacy or commercial success until measured with a defined method and sample.
