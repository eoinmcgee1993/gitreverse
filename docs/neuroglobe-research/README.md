# Neuroglobe reverse-engineering project

**Status:** research and implementation starter, not a validated commercial launch.  
**Target:** https://neuroglobe.store/ and its publicly listed product page.  
**Audit date:** 2026-10-12 UTC.  
**Working product direction:** *Attention Field Manual: 30 Days to Build Better Defaults* (Digital Renaissance / DegenDev working brand).

## Files
- `01-research-dossier.md`: observed page structure, offer, funnel, unknowns and evidence limits.
- `02-source-claim-ledger.md`: source ledger and evidence classification for substantive claims.
- `03-product-spec.md`: differentiated programme and 30-day curriculum.
- `04-commercial-assets.md`: persona, positioning, original sales copy, emails, social plan, pricing tests and QA.
- `05-implementation-report.md`: code audit, branch, schema/retry implementation and validation limitations.
- `../../lib/research-output-schema.ts`: Zod schema for provenance-rich research records.
- `../../lib/research-retry.ts`: bounded retry helper for transient research failures.
- `../../scripts/test-research-output-schema.ts`: node:test checks for schema and retry behaviour.

## Evidence labels
- **Observed:** directly visible in the retrieved public page or repository file.
- **Source-backed:** supported by a cited publication or official documentation.
- **Interpretation:** a reasoned reading of evidence, not directly observed.
- **Unknown:** not checked, not accessible through the available tool, or not verified.

## Use and limits
This is competitive analysis, not a copy of Neuroglobe's product. Do not reuse its text, graphics, source files, page design, or distinctive brand. The new programme is educational habit support, not diagnosis or treatment. A 30-day schedule is a programme format, not a claim that a habit or neurobiological change is guaranteed in 30 days.

No purchase was made and no checkout, authenticated account, customer data, or restricted area was accessed. REA's public setup and browser-analysis documentation were reviewed; REA was not installed or run from this hosted session. See the implementation report.
